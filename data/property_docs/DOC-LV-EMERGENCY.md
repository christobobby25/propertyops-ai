# Lakeview Emergency Escalation Policy

## Immediate danger
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-EMERGENCY-S01 -->

For fire, smoke, a suspected gas leak, or an immediate threat to life, move to safety and contact local emergency services. Do not wait for a chatbot or a maintenance ticket. Do not attempt equipment repairs.

## Maintenance emergency
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-EMERGENCY-S02 -->

Uncontrolled active water flow and an exterior unit door that cannot be secured are maintenance emergencies. Contact the Lakeview demo on-call maintenance channel immediately. A contained dripping faucet is routine. A contained sink leak without continuing damage is routine at Lakeview.

## After-hours escalation
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-EMERGENCY-S03 -->

Maintenance emergencies use the on-call channel at all hours. Urgent requests submitted outside office hours also use the Lakeview demo on-call maintenance channel. Routine requests are reviewed during office hours. Ticket creation does not confirm dispatch.
