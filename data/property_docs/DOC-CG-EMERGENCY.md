# Cedar Grove Emergency Escalation Policy

## Immediate danger
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-EMERGENCY-S01 -->

For fire, smoke, a suspected gas leak, or an immediate threat to life, move to safety and contact local emergency services. Do not wait for a chatbot or a maintenance ticket. Do not attempt equipment repairs.

## Maintenance emergency
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-EMERGENCY-S02 -->

Uncontrolled active water flow and an exterior unit door that cannot be secured are maintenance emergencies. Contact the Cedar Grove demo on-call maintenance channel immediately. A contained dripping faucet is routine. A contained sink leak without continuing damage is urgent.

## After-hours escalation
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-EMERGENCY-S03 -->

Maintenance emergencies use the on-call channel at all hours. Urgent and routine requests may be submitted at any time. Routine requests are reviewed during office hours. A ticket does not confirm that a technician has been dispatched.
