# Lakeview HVAC Maintenance Policy

## Cooling priority
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-HVAC-S01 -->

At Lakeview, an AC outage with a resident-reported indoor temperature of 82 degrees Fahrenheit or higher is urgent. An AC outage below 82 degrees is routine. Ask for the indoor temperature when it is not provided. An AC outage alone is not automatically an emergency.

## Safe initial checks
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-HVAC-S02 -->

Residents may verify thermostat mode and setpoint and replace thermostat batteries when accessible. Do not instruct residents to open equipment, repair wiring, or handle refrigerant. Smoke, burning smells, or immediate danger follow the emergency escalation policy.

## Response targets
<!-- section_id: DOC-LV-HVAC-S03 -->

Urgent HVAC requests have a target initial contact within 4 hours. Routine HVAC requests have a target initial contact within 1 business day. These are contact targets, not guaranteed arrival or repair times. Scheduling must be checked in current work-order records.
