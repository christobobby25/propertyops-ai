# Cedar Grove HVAC Maintenance Policy

## Cooling priority
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-HVAC-S01 -->

At Cedar Grove, an AC outage with a resident-reported indoor temperature of 85 degrees Fahrenheit or higher is urgent. An AC outage below 85 degrees is routine. Ask for the indoor temperature when it is not provided. An AC outage alone is not automatically an emergency.

## Safe initial checks
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-HVAC-S02 -->

Residents may check the thermostat mode, setpoint, and whether thermostat batteries need replacement. Do not instruct residents to open electrical panels, handle refrigerant, or repair equipment. Smoke, burning smells, or immediate danger follow the emergency escalation policy.

## Response targets
<!-- section_id: DOC-CG-HVAC-S03 -->

Urgent HVAC requests have a target initial contact within 2 hours. Routine HVAC requests have a target initial contact within 2 business days. These are contact targets, not guaranteed arrival or repair times. Check current work-order records for scheduling.
